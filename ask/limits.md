# /ask: what it can and can't answer yet

Works (any league in leagues.json, last ~60 days, times in ET):
- Who won a tour (by date and time; nearest tour that day if no exact match). Includes team players when the tour's TEAMS page lists them.
- Who ran (TD) a tour, its game type/format, entry, whether it was cancelled.
- How many tours ran in a league on a day or in a date range.
- How many tours a player won (and which) on a day or in a range.
- List of a day's tours and winners.
- How many tours a player played (entered), with 1st/2nd/3rd counts, bux won and last entry, all time or for a date range. Any player-history question with no period (including "when did X last play") searches the player's entire log, not 60 days. Source: the league's public Ladderbux log (cgi6.igl.net, or plus2.igl.net for MyLeague Plus leagues such as AMS and Just4Fun) (full history for that league; Ochwiay's starts Jun 20, 2026).
- Tours-played questions that say any/all/every league, or name no league, search every league in leagues.json and answer per league (leagues with none are named). Naming a league searches only that league.
- When a player last won a tour in a league (searches back up to 60 days).

- Which tours a player played in a period (today, yesterday, this/last week, a date, up to 31 days): "what tours did X play today", "how many tours did X play this week", "did X win any tours". Lists each tour with start time (ET), board label, TD and result (won / 2nd / 3rd / played), plus the partner and team name in partner tours, across every league unless one is named. Source: the player's Ladderbux log (which tours charged an entry fee, and placing) joined to the league calendar by tour id, and the tour's TEAMS page for partners and not-yet-finished registrations. Aliases in leagues.json are honored.
- Who played in a tour: "who played in the 6:15" (a start time, today unless a date is given): the entrant list from the tour's TEAMS page, with teams for partner tours and the winner.
- Leaderboard (no player name needed): "who won the most CUT tours at Diamond Spaders in October", "most wins", "top 5 winners", "who hosted the most", optionally filtered by game type (the six official board labels: CUT, SINGLES, PAB SUI PASS 2, REG PARTNERS, SUI PASS 2, LOSER WINS; case-insensitive) or TD/host. Default league is Diamond Spaders. Periods: month names (most recent such month), "this week", "last month" (the previous calendar month), "today", "yesterday", and date ranges. The answer names the leader (or a tie), the win count, and a top-5 list (ties at the cutoff are included). Partner tours credit each winner; cancelled/hidden tours are excluded. Game-type matching is exact on the official board label (CUT includes the former CUT MIRRORS; SUI PASS 2 includes the former RANDOM; every PAB is PAB SUI PASS 2). When MyLeague's calendar no longer lists older days, Diamond Spaders days are filled from published daily reports when those exist.
- Who played the most tours on a single day, when that day's daily report is available (all game types together). A month-long "who played the most" is not available.
- A player's success rate by game variation (the six official board labels only: CUT, SINGLES, PAB SUI PASS 2, REG PARTNERS, SUI PASS 2, LOSER WINS), at tour level and at game level. A tour win is being posted as the tournament winner. A game is one table/match the player's team played inside that tour's bracket (the W-L on the MyLeague TEAMS page); byes are not counted. Questions that say "games", "matches", or "hands" (and "games, not tours") get the game record, e.g. "8 wins in 12 games (66.7%)". Questions that say "tours" get the tour record. If the question does not say which, both are shown. "I"/"me"/"my"/"mine" is the signed-in site user's MyLeague handle (the username on their Diamond Spaders account); a named player also works for anyone in the tour data. Periods: today, this/last week, this/last month, a month name (October, November), last N days, and date ranges. The answer leads with the best (or worst, if asked) game type, then the table. Related phrasings: best/worst game type, win rate, record by variation, "what variation have I had the most success with, percentage wise," "of games, not tours, what's my winning percentage this month PAB SUI PASS 2." Partner-tour wins count if the player was on the winning team; the game W-L is the team's match record, shared by both partners. Tour rates count only finished Diamond Spaders tours with a posted winner. Game rates count finished tours that have a TEAMS-page record, even if a winner has not been posted yet. Source: each tour's checked-in entrant list (TEAMS page) joined to board labels and posted winners; kept up to date by the same updater that publishes the board. Coverage starts when those entry lists were first stored (currently mid-September 2026) and includes this month in full. Many mid-to-late September 2026 tours have an entry list but no posted winner in the file, so September tour rates skip those tours; October and November lists include posted winners. Game records are backfilled from the same TEAMS pages (including October and November).


Not reachable / not supported yet:
- "I"/"me"/"my"/"mine" is the signed-in site user's MyLeague handle. For any other player, name them.
- Played-tour dates/times are when the entry was made (Ladderbux log), not the tour's start; the log has no partner names. Tours entered for free (no entry fee) would not be counted.
- Individual hand or trick scores, and in-game point totals. (Game-level W-L — each table/match inside a tour — is available on win-rate questions.) Placings below 3rd are not in the log (shown as "played, did not place"). Entrant lists are one tour per question.
- Ladder standings, ratings, Ladderbux balances, player profiles.
- Leagues not in leagues.json (add them there).
- Tour names that exist only on the tour's own page (the feed has the format text, e.g. "2 VS 2").
- More than 60 days back for winner/calendar questions (player tours-played history has no limit). Leaderboards can reach a named month a bit further back when daily reports exist (Diamond Spaders, roughly the last few months).
- "Who played the most" over more than one day (no complete entrant lists for a whole month).
- Player wins inside team tours where the feed lists only the team name (counted only when the TEAMS page is checked, which is done for single-tour questions).
